# UNDERBODY SKID PLATE ASSEMBLY 🛡️🚗

## 1. Overview

The **Underbody Skid Plate Assembly** project focuses on designing a multi‑piece sheet‑metal protection system for the vehicle underbody using 3D CAD tools.  
The assembly combines a main skid plate, intermediate brackets, and an upper reinforcement cover to protect critical components while remaining lightweight and easy to manufacture.

---

## 2. Aim 🎯

- Develop a **bolt‑on skid plate module** that mounts to existing chassis/subframe points and shields vulnerable underbody components.  
- Use CAD to define the **plate geometry, bends, vents, and brackets** to achieve stiffness, clearance, and straightforward fabrication.  
- Produce a complete **3D assembly and 2D drawing set** suitable for manufacturing and portfolio presentation.

---

## 3. Tech Stack & Tools 🧰

### Software

- 3D CAD: **CATIA V5 **

### Core Skills

- Sheet‑metal‑style modelling with flanges, bends, and formed features.  
- Bracket and spacer design for underbody mounting.  
- Assembly constraints and interference checks in CAD.  
- 2D drawing and flat‑pattern creation for laser cutting and bending.

---

## 4. Assembly Architecture 🧱

The skid plate assembly consists of:

- **Main skid plate (yellow)**  
  - Primary protective panel with front and side flanges for stiffness and edge protection.  
  - Includes elongated cut‑outs for bolt access, drainage, and weight reduction.

- **Intermediate mounting bracket (orange)**  
  - Bolted to the main plate and provides stepped mounting points to the vehicle structure.  
  - Aligns the skid plate at the correct height and angle relative to the underbody.

- **Upper reinforcement / cover plate (red)**  
  - Mounted on the raised rear section of the main plate.  
  - Adds local stiffness and can act as an additional shield for specific components or fasteners.

- **Fasteners and spacers**  
  - Standard bolts, washers, and optional spacers connecting the plate and brackets to chassis points.  
  - Hole patterns designed for easy assembly and repeatable positioning.

---

## 5. Modelling Workflow 🧩

1. **Define underbody envelope**  
   - Capture the clearance envelope for the protected components and the available mounting points in CAD.  
   - Set target ground clearance and approach angle for the skid plate.

2. **Main plate design**  
   - Sketch and extrude the base profile, then add front and side flanges using sheet‑metal features.  
   - Create elongated slots and holes for mounting, drainage, and access.

3. **Bracket and reinforcement modelling**  
   - Design the intermediate bracket to bridge between the plate and chassis mounting faces.  
   - Model the upper reinforcement plate to match the raised area, using bends and holes aligned with the main plate.

4. **Assembly and clearance checks**  
   - Assemble all components, apply mates, and verify there are no clashes with the underbody or fasteners.  
   - Adjust flange angles, bracket offsets, and plate curvature if required.

5. **Detailing & manufacturing outputs**  
   - Generate **2D drawings** for each part with bend lines, angles, radii, and material thickness.  
   - Create flat‑patterns/DXF files for laser cutting and press‑brake operations.  
   - Prepare an **exploded view and BOM** for assembly instructions.

---

## 6. Key Features & CAD Highlights 🌟

- **Multi‑piece sheet‑metal assembly** with a main plate, mounting bracket, and reinforcement cover for tuned stiffness and modularity.  
- **Vent and slot geometry** designed directly in CAD for airflow, drainage, and reduced mass without compromising protection.  
- **Manufacturing‑aware modelling**, with realistic thicknesses, bend radii, and flat‑pattern outputs ready for fabrication.  
- **Service‑friendly design** with access holes and removable components to support maintenance without full removal of the system.
